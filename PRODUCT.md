## Platform

web

## Users

Primary audience for the case-study surface: **creators and job seekers** evaluating
Work Beyond Borders as a place to work. They want to see how campaigns are actually
run and what the standard of work looks like before applying or taking a brief.

The wider site also serves prospective client brands (a marketing lead or founder
deciding whether to hire WBB) and existing clients. The careers page carries thirteen
open roles, so recruitment is a standing job of the site, not an afterthought.

## Product Purpose

Work Beyond Borders (WBB) is a remote-first influencer and digital marketing agency.
It runs creator-led campaigns, paid media and social programmes for brands, and
publishes its results, activities and open roles on this site. Success for the site is
a visitor who understands what WBB does, believes it works, and either gets in touch
or applies.

## Positioning

**Remote-first economics.** A distributed team across the Philippines delivers
agency-grade performance work at costs a traditional agency structure cannot match.
The campaign results are the proof that the distributed model produces real commercial
outcomes, not just cheaper hours.

## Operating Context

- Remote-first, worldwide; team distributed across the Philippines.
- Regional team meetups (Davao, Iloilo, General Santos) are a real part of how the
  company operates and are documented on the Activities page.
- Campaign work spans creator sourcing, paid amplification, and performance reporting

## Product Principles

1. **Report honestly, including the caveats.** The source document itself marks the
   13.2x weekend as a signal rather than proof and the Google halo as correlation, not
   a controlled incrementality test. Carry those caveats rather than burying them.
2. **The system is the story, not the hero video.** Results came from continuously
   supplying creator variation, not from one viral asset.
3. **Show the work, not just the outcome.** Creators and applicants judge WBB on how
   campaigns are run.
4. **Remote-first is the mechanism, not a disclaimer.** The distributed team is why the
   economics work.
